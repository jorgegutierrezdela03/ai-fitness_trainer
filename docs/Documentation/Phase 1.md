# Phase 1: Definition, Initial Baseline and Evaluation

**Objective:** To develop a reproducible core system that generates personalized training plans based on user profiles and requests, without relying on external knowledge, and to establish an evaluation protocol for the generated results.

## P1-01 — Define Project Scope and Requirements

Define the scope, target users, functional requirements, and constraints of the AI Fitness Trainer baseline.

### P1-01.1 — Define Target Population and Project Scope

#### Objective
Define the target users, training objectives, and initial boundaries of the AI Personal Trainer.

#### Target Population
Adults aged 18 or older who perform or intend to perform exercise in a conventional gym, with different experience levels and general fitness goals.

#### Inclusion Criteria
- Generally healthy individuals without conditions requiring clinical exercise prescription.
- Beginners, intermediate, and advanced recreational gym users.
- Users seeking personalized training for general fitness, strength, hypertrophy, muscular endurance, cardiovascular health, or body composition.

#### Exclusion Criteria
- Elite athletes requiring specialized training.
- Marathon, triathlon, or competitive endurance preparation.
- Competitive bodybuilding and specialized calisthenics.
- Clinical rehabilitation or medically supervised exercise.
- Nutritional planning and dietary interventions.

#### Training Context
The system will support conventional gym-based training using machines, dumbbells, barbells, free weights, cables, and cardiovascular equipment.

#### Supported Training Objectives
- Strength.
- Hypertrophy.
- Muscular endurance.
- General fitness.
- Fat loss through exercise.
- Cardiovascular fitness.
- Combined resistance and cardiovascular training.

### P1-01.2 — Define User Profile Specifications

#### Objective
Define the user information required to generate personalized training plans.

#### User Profile

##### Basic Information
- Age: Required.
- Sex: Optional.
- Height: Optional.
- Weight: Optional.

Height and weight will remain optional until their usefulness for different training objectives is evaluated.

##### Training Experience
Required. Three predefined levels:
- Beginner.
- Intermediate.
- Advanced.

##### Training Goals
Required. Select up to two primary objectives:
- Strength.
- Hypertrophy.
- Fat loss.
- Cardiovascular fitness.
- Muscular endurance.
- General fitness.

##### Training Availability
- Available training days per week: Required.
- Available session duration: Required.

##### Training Preferences and Restrictions
Optional questions covering:
- Exercises the user prefers to avoid.
- Equipment or gym limitations.
- Preferred training style.

The system will assume a conventional gym environment while allowing users to specify preferences or limitations related to their training environment.

##### Physical Limitations
Required Yes/No question:

**Do you have any physical limitations that may affect your training?**

If Yes:
- Select affected body areas (shoulder, elbow, wrist, back, hip, knee, ankle, or other).
- Provide an optional free-text description.

The system will use this information to identify and respect training restrictions without providing clinical or medical exercise prescription.

#### Data Considerations
- Prioritize structured inputs and predefined options.
- Use optional fields when information is not essential for initial plan generation.
- Minimize the collection of personal and health-related information.
- Initially use synthetic user profiles for experimental evaluation.

## P1-02 — Define Output Format

Define a standardized output structure for the training plans generated by the LLM.


### P1-02.1 — Define Training Plan Output Structure

#### Objective
Define a standardized structure for the training plans generated by the LLM, specifying the information required to represent training sessions, exercises, and their associated instructions.

#### 1. Session Header

Each training session will contain:

- Descriptive title.
- Main session focus.
- Estimated total duration in minutes.

The following metadata will also be included in the final output:

- Session ID.
- User ID.
- Generation date and time.

**Metadata considerations:**

Session ID, User ID, and generation timestamp must be generated and managed by the Python backend, not by the LLM. The backend will add this information to the generated plan.

These fields will support storage, identification, and future session history analysis. They will not be part of the initial training plan evaluation.

#### 2. Warm-up

Optional section that may include:

- **General activation:** Joint mobility exercises or light cardiovascular activity.
- **Specific warm-up:** Activities related to the main exercises.
- **Warm-up sets:** Additional preparation sets for the first main exercises, when appropriate.

The inclusion and content of the warm-up will depend on the session and its exercises.

#### 3. Main Training Block

A structured and ordered list of exercises included in the session.

Each exercise will contain the following information:

**General Information**
- Exercise name.
- Exercise modality:
  - Strength.
  - Cardio.
  - Mobility.
- Exercise priority within the session:
  - Primary.
  - Secondary.
  - Accessory.
- Primary muscle group, when applicable.
- Secondary muscle groups, when applicable.

The priority classification will help represent the importance of each exercise in relation to the session focus and facilitate the evaluation of exercise selection and ordering.

**Exercise Parameters**

Training parameters will depend on the exercise modality.

- **Strength:** Number of sets, target repetitions or repetition range, rest time in seconds, and target intensity using RPE (1–10).
- **Cardio:** Duration and cardiovascular intensity.
- **Mobility:** Duration or repetitions, depending on the exercise.

Cardiovascular intensity will initially use three descriptive levels:
- Low.
- Moderate.
- High.

The exercise modality must be explicitly specified so that the output can be validated against the corresponding parameters.

**Load Management**

The LLM will not prescribe mandatory absolute weights in the initial version.

Users will select an appropriate load to reach the target intensity specified in the training plan.

Recording actual loads and adapting future recommendations based on previous sessions will remain potential future developments.

**Exercise Instructions**

Each exercise will include brief, exercise-specific technical instructions.

- Mandatory for every exercise.
- Maximum of two instructions per exercise.
- Relevant to the exercise and adapted to the user's restrictions when applicable.

#### 4. Additional Cardio

Optional section for complementary cardiovascular training when relevant to the user's goals or session requirements.

It may include:
- Activity type.
- Duration.
- Target cardiovascular intensity.
- Additional instructions, when appropriate.

This section is independent of cardiovascular exercises that may already appear in the main training block.

#### 5. Cool-down

Optional section that may include:

- Mobility exercises or stretching.
- Estimated duration.
- Additional instructions.

Its inclusion will depend on the session requirements.

#### 6. General Session Notes

Optional free-text section for complementary information about the training session.

It may include:
- General execution recommendations.
- Effort-related guidance.
- Progression reminders.
- Additional considerations related to the session.

General notes must not replace exercise-specific instructions or compensate for restrictions that should already have been respected during exercise selection.

#### General Considerations

- The output structure must accommodate different exercise modalities and their specific training parameters.
- Not all exercises require the same training fields.
- The estimated session duration must be adapted to the time available to the user.
- The user's usual availability may be used as a reference when no specific duration is provided for the current session.
- The initial user profile and session-specific requests will be defined separately as part of the LLM input structure.
- Persistent identifiers and timestamps must be managed by the backend rather than generated by the LLM.
- Exercise identifiers must be resolved by the backend against an exercise catalogue when available. The LLM must not invent persistent database identifiers.
- Absolute load prescription and longitudinal training adaptation are outside the scope of the initial version.
- The conceptual structure may be refined during implementation if necessary, without changing its fundamental organization.
